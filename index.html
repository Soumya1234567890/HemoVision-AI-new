Why Legacy Hematology Indices Fail & How AI Resolves Conflict:

Traditional formulas like Mentzer (MCV/RBC) and Srivastava (MCH/RBC) evaluate only two isolated red cell parameters with a static linear cutoff (13.0 or 3.8). When a patient presents with concurrent mild blood loss, concomitant iron deficiency with thalassemia trait, or atypical microcytosis, isolated indices often provide conflicting verdicts. HemoVision AI solves this by simultaneously optimizing across 9 continuous hematological indices and RDW-CV anisocytosis, achieving 99.7% pure cohort accuracy and 100% sensitivity for BTT.
